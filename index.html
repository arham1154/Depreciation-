Reducing balance never hits zero. A small value always stays on the books until you sell or scrap the asset — that's normal, and you settle it in the year you dispose of it.

Tax saved isn't profit. It's tax you didn't have to pay because the write-off cut your taxable income. You still spent the money on the asset.

Filing works slightly differently. Income tax pools every asset at the same rate into one block, so your return won't show one asset on its own. The yearly amounts are the same — this view is for planning a purchase. Run it past your CA before you file.
